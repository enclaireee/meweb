# World options (assistant proposals, round 8)

Common constraints (all decided): layered flat papercut shadowbox style (v4 art direction, see `reference-v4-art-direction.md`); epic and cinematic like aksarakala; "the world opens itself" as a signature move; aimless exploration; neutral fantasy; one continuous scroll; two lanes (main = the experience, projects = reverse side of the sheet, entered by clicking an object); projects are modular registry entries and are **places or objects that belong in the world**, never custom showcases; light through cut paper is the hero effect.

---

## Option A: The Contour Canyon (descent through strata)
**Pitch.** A vast landscape built like a topographic paper model: stacked contour layers of card. The scroll is a **descent** into an impossible canyon whose walls are strata, each stratum a different colour and time of day.

**Opening.** A rim at dusk. A tiny lit watchtower on layered cliffs; the canyon drops away into depth. One line: "Everything down there was folded once."

**How it opens.** Layers part like curtains as you descend; each depth band reveals a new band of world with its own palette and light (dawn strata, green terraces, blue lantern abyss, ember core).

**Epic moments.** A colossal paper creature sliding through the canyon below you; a bridge of light across the abyss; the core, a single sun-sized lamp lit through vellum.

**Projects lane (reverse side).** Flip the sheet and you climb the opposite wall: each project is a lit cave, balcony, or ledge cut into the cliff face.

**Strength.** Strongest "layered paper" identity (real topographic paper art). Depth is literally the structure, so parallax is the story.
**Risk.** Mostly vertical and one direction; needs variety in horizontal composition.

---

## Option B: The Walking City (a journey on a moving world)
**Pitch.** A colossal paper city that **walks** across an enormous landscape. You are riding in it. The city is the world; the landscape outside slides past.

**Opening.** Night. The city's silhouette towers on layered legs across a moonlit plain, thousands of windows lit through cut holes. It takes a step and the world shakes.

**How it opens.** Districts unfold as pop-up kirigami when you reach them (buildings rise from flat sheets, bridges extend). Outside changes: desert, storm, ice, sea of clouds, so mood changes sharply by region while the city stays.

**Epic moments.** The city crossing a river like a titan; a storm front lit from inside the city; docking with another walking city.

**Projects lane (reverse side).** The underbelly: engine halls, workshops, hangars. Each project is a room, shop, or machine. Adding a project means adding a room.

**Strength.** Best for modularity (add a building, shop, or district). Journey feels natural for aimless exploration. Easy to place content without feeling like a showcase.
**Risk.** Familiar archetype (walking castle/city); must be given a distinct paper identity to feel fresh.

---

## Option C: The Hollow Mountain (bigger on the inside)
**Pitch.** A small, quiet mountain at night that **unfolds like origami** and turns out to contain an endless world.

**Opening.** One lone paper mountain under the moon, a single warm window. A line: "It looked small from outside."

**How it opens.** The mountain unfolds; the scroll crosses a threshold (a fold) and the scale flips: the inside is a vast cavern world with its own paper sun, forest canopies layered upward, a lantern-star vault, rivers of light. Further scroll keeps revealing that each space contains a bigger one.

**Epic moments.** The scale reversal at the first fold; the first sight of the inner sun; a space so big the lanterns look like constellations.

**Projects lane (reverse side).** The outer shell: from outside, the mountain's skin shows hollows and doors. Each project is a chamber in the wall that you open and step into.

**Strength.** The most literal "the world opens itself". Scale shock is the signature and delivers WOW even in a screenshot. Fold mechanic is the central idea, not decoration.
**Risk.** The scale reversal has to be executed perfectly; weaker on "journey across a landscape".

| | A Canyon | B Walking City | C Hollow Mountain |
|---|---|---|---|
| WOW source | Depth and descent | Scale of a moving city | Scale reversal |
| Fits "world opens itself" | Layers part | Districts unfold | Mountain unfolds, space flips |
| Modular projects | Ledges/caves | Rooms/shops/buildings | Chambers |
| Variety of mood | Strata bands | Landscapes outside | Inner regions |
| Risk | One-directional | Familiar archetype | Needs flawless scale reversal |
| Assistant lean | | Strong modularity | Strongest WOW |

Mixing is allowed (e.g. a Hollow Mountain that is also moving, or a Canyon whose depths contain a Walking City).
