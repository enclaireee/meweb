# Concept: The Hollow Mountain

> "It looked small from outside."

A portfolio built as **one paper-art world**, experienced as **one continuous scroll**. The world is the draw; the work is found inside it.

> **Note:** where this file describes project chambers as rooms with their own objects, or roles/skills/awards as objects in the scene, `decisions.md` (Build decisions) overrides it: art is world-only, content goes on paper cards and labels.

## Why
- The previous version (Paper River v4) was calm and nice but not a showcase and not WOW: a small fixed frame, one palette, one slow idea, work hidden in small tags.
- Reference: https://aksarakala.vercel.app/ . What makes it WOW: every section is a different world with its own mood; huge hero images; very little, very large text; living details (particles, glow, nav, sound); plain native scroll. We take the *energy*, not the marketing-page structure.
- Owner direction: not a "CV presentation". One world, with the portfolio integrated into it.

## The idea
A quiet paper mountain at night turns out to contain a vast world, and that world contains bigger ones. **Scale and light invert at each fold**: small, cool and quiet outside; vast, warm and alive inside. The visitor's sense of size is reset again and again.

## The content rule
> If the portfolio did not exist, would this object still make sense in this world?

If not, it is a showcase element and does not belong. Projects, skills and experience appear as places and objects that **would be there anyway**.

## Two lanes
- **Main lane:** the WOW journey (10 beats, see `world-hollow-mountain.md`). Complete on its own.
- **Projects lane:** the reverse side of the world. Fork objects in the main lane (glowing doors, tabs, flaps) invite a click; the world **flips inside-out** and you see the mountain's outer shell, whose hollows are **chambers**, one per project. Exit flips back to the exact spot.
- **Modular:** a project is one registry entry; the shell builds chambers from the registry. See `tech-spec.md`.

## Look
- **Layered flat papercut shadowbox**: every sheet one flat colour, no gradients inside a sheet, straight scissor-cut edges, details as holes, depth from overlap, baked soft shadows, edge lights, light shining through the cuts. Rules in `reference-v4-art-direction.md`.
- **Colour is a WOW lever**: outside cool moonlit with rare warm points; inside warm and golden; the Second Fold flips again.
- **Inside:** nature and ruins mixed (roots through stairs, trees through arches, shrines half swallowed).
- **Creatures:** one signature creature per region, few poses each, drama from scale and light.
- **Figure:** a small silent traveller that walks as you scroll, always tiny, always lit by a rim so it reads on both palettes.

## Motion and scroll
- Native scroll, no hijack. A **vocabulary of transitions**, each beat boundary using a different one: unfold, zoom-through, layers part like curtains, pull-back, flip. Every transition is scroll-driven and **reversible**.
- Text: one big line per scene plus a tiny label, as in the reference. All text is real HTML.
- Sound: optional ambient and paper foley, off by default.

## Principles
1. **One world, one scroll.** Nothing breaks out of it.
2. **Surprise, hold, surprise.** One hero effect per beat; no two beats share a composition.
3. **Light is the story.** Warm light is rare and meaningful.
4. **Flat sheets, real depth.** No fake 3D inside art; depth by layering.
5. **Good + light.** Beautiful and fast; mid-range phones included.
6. **Craft is the product.** Edges, shadows and timing good enough to be admired.
7. **Readable underneath.** Real HTML, reduced-motion and no-JS fallbacks, deep links.

## What it is not
- Not a presentation of a CV, not per-project showcases, not a marketing page, not a 3D engine demo.
- Not the v4 river, frame or step-based navigation.
