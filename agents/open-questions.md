# Open questions

Only things that are **still undecided**. Decided items live in `decisions.md`.

## Content (blocks the projects lane and copy)
1. **Content inventory:** list of projects, experience, skills, and what media exists (screenshots, video, none). Decides how many chambers and what objects they need.
2. **Contact:** which channels (email, LinkedIn, CV download, others) go in The Window.
3. **Language:** English only, or EN + ID toggle?

## World and art details
4. **Small figure design:** look, silhouette, walk cycle, how it reads on cool and warm palettes.
5. **Creature list:** confirm or change the proposed one-per-region creatures.
6. **Beat copy:** the big line and small label for each of the 10 beats.
7. **Interaction depth beyond scroll:** only atmosphere (cursor light, parallax), or small playful interactions (light a lantern, touch a creature)?
8. **How literal is the object-to-content mapping:** obvious signage vs discovered metaphor?
9. **Fork objects:** what they look like in each region and how they hint "click me".
10. **Project chambers:** the generic chamber template and how media (images/video) sit inside a chamber.
11. **Mobile composition:** portrait layout of every beat (needs per-beat design).

## Technical
12. **Spikes** S1 to S5 in `tech-spec.md`: do they meet the performance targets? Is WebGL needed for the First Fold?
13. **Exact performance budget** after the first spike measurements.
14. **Browser support floor** (which versions).
15. **Sound assets:** source and licensing for ambient and paper foley.

## Process
16. **When to start building** and the first deliverable (suggested: spike S1 plus beats 0 and 1).
