PT Dyvia Sinergi Hutama supplies industrial instrumentation. They needed a
company profile site. I built it from a design they supplied, on Next.js and
Tailwind, deployed to Vercel.

There is no hidden complexity to report and I am not going to invent any. The
job was to be fast, correct, and out of the way: the metadata is right, the
Open Graph previews render properly when someone pastes the link into a chat,
and the images go out as WebP through `next/image` so the pages load quickly on
a phone in a warehouse.

That is the whole project. Some client work is a puzzle; this one was a
delivery, and doing a delivery well means it is boring to write about.
