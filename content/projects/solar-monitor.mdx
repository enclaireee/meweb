A solar-powered LED lamp is a solved problem. A solar-powered LED lamp that
tells you how well it is doing right now, under this particular sky, is a
slightly more interesting one. Sunmeter is the final project for Embedded
Systems, and the whole point is the measurement, not the light.

## Measuring your own supply

An INA219 sits on the panel output measuring current and voltage; an LDR gives
a rough reference for how much light is arriving. The Arduino computes live
efficiency from those and puts it on a 0.96" I²C OLED, which means you can
tilt the panel and watch the number move.

## The short version

- INA219 current/voltage sensing with an LDR light reference
- Arduino firmware computing live efficiency under changing light
- Photovoltaic panel, LED load and sensor array integrated into one unit
- Live metrics on a 0.96″ I²C OLED
