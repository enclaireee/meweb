KOMAT is a national mathematics competition run by Universitas Katolik
Parahyangan. I built the platform it runs on: the participant-facing site,
and the admin dashboard behind it that handles registration and payment for
entrants from across Indonesia.

## The part that mattered

A competition site is quiet for months and then has one week where everyone
shows up at once, pays, and expects a confirmation. That week is the whole
project. The public pages are the easy half.

<Figure
  want="work/komat-unpar/admin.png"
  alt="The admin dashboard listing registrations and payment states"
  caption="The admin side, which is where the actual work happened: registrations, payment states, and the ability to fix a record when someone paid twice."
/>

It shipped on Next.js 15 with Tailwind v4 and Framer Motion, deployed to
Vercel with continuous delivery, and I maintained it in production through the
full competition cycle rather than handing it over at launch.
