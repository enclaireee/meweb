Attention training usually asks you to sit still and try harder. Refocus
inverts that. The game reads your attention off your scalp and makes itself
harder the moment it finds any, so the only way to keep playing is to stay
locked in. It was built as a non-pharmacological approach to ADHD therapy,
and the whole loop runs live: scalp to screen, no recording, no offline pass.

## Getting a signal worth using

A NeuroSky MindWave Mobile 2 streams raw EEG over Bluetooth. *Raw* is the
operative word: a single dry electrode on the forehead picks up jaw clenches,
blinks, and the mains hum about as enthusiastically as it picks up anything
cortical. Most of the work on this project was separating those.

The pipeline runs a DB-6 discrete wavelet transform to break the stream into
frequency bands, then a five-model GMM ensemble classifies attention state
from what comes out. The number that actually leaves the pipeline is the ratio
of low-beta power to theta power, computed per window.

```python
# the control variable, computed per EEG window
ratio = band_power(low_beta) / band_power(theta)
difficulty.set_target(clamp(k * ratio, MIN_LVL, MAX_LVL))
```

## Closing the loop

That ratio drives difficulty in a Godot space shooter. Difficulty doesn't jump
to match it — it pursues it, so a moment of distraction doesn't hand you an
easy game instantly, and a moment of focus doesn't punish you instantly
either. The pursuit is the part that makes it playable rather than annoying.
